# Miso landing-page design drafts

Generated October 31, 2026 using the built-in image-generation tool.
These are raster visual explorations, not implemented pages. The live application
has not been changed. Product-preview figures are illustrative. Marketing copy,
small labels, feature links, and exact colors need review before implementation.

Palette drawn from the current app: warm tan `#FEF8EC`, paper `#FCF5E5`, cream
`#FFFCF6`, and sky blue `#C3E0F2`. Proposed supporting colors: deeper blue
`#427DA3` and dark navy `#243D4B` for readable text and controls.

## A — Quiet editorial

File: `option-a-quiet-editorial.png`.

Prompt direction: A polished desktop marketing landing page for Miso with a
tan-dominant split hero, generous whitespace, readable humanist sans-serif type,
a pixel wordmark/cat, sky-blue stepped buttons, and a restrained product preview.
Headline: “Study a little. Remember a lot.” Use pixel details as accents. Follow
the hero with open feature columns for notes-to-cards, active recall, and spaced
review. Professional productivity-product tone with a small amount of pixel charm.

## B — Sky studio

File: `option-b-sky-studio.png`.

Prompt direction: A distinctly different centered landing page for Miso, with a
sky-blue hero, sparse pixel clouds, a tan product window, and a stepped transition
to the tan lower section. Headline: “A calmer way to learn.” Make the study input
preview prominent and add a small cream-white cat reading a blue book. Follow it
with a three-step notes-to-knowledge process. Maintain professional typography,
clear hierarchy, and flat pixel accents. No multiple-choice UI.

The mockups intentionally explore composition and brand direction. The generated
“Show hint” and “Notes” controls in B, and the timing claim in A, are not a
commitment to additional functionality or a performance guarantee; replace these
with existing capabilities and verified copy in an implementation.
